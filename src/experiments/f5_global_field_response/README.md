## 🇬🇧 English Description

This experiment explores the **field-level projection**
of **F5 — Biosignatures (Astrobiology and Cosmic Meaning Traces)**.

The theoretical hypothesis underlying F5 is that regions with:
- high free energy flow  
- low local entropy  
- long-range correlations  

may carry **meaning-like signatures**,
even in the absence of an explicit observer.

In this framework, meaning (A) is defined as observer-dependent,
while meaning potential (PA) may remain high without observation:

A = 0  (no observer)  
PA ≫ 0 (high potential)

This experiment:
- does **not** claim to detect life  
- does **not** measure cosmic meaning directly  

Instead, it aggregates responses from multiple local fields
and computes a **global field response**
as a minimal indicator of
collective alignment or coherence.

The resulting average:
- is not a semantic quantity by itself  
- but serves as a primitive observable for
  synchrony and correlated structure
  across distributed subsystems.

Thus, F5 functions as the conceptual closure
of the experimental sequence,
extending the energy–information–meaning framework
from localized systems
to **global and potentially cosmic scales**.
